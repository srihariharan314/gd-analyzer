Selected Topic Module

Will AI Replace Human Jobs or Create New Ones?

Grounding facts...
Artificial Intelligence Intermediate

Topic Overview

The rise of generative AI and automated decision-making has ignited debate over technological unemployment versus the creation of higher-value roles and augmentation.

Essential Discussion Anchors (Key Points)

  • Routine and repetitive cognitive tasks (data entry, basic coding, drafting) face significant automation.
  • Historical technological revolutions (Industrial, Internet) eliminated roles but created net more jobs over time.
  • Reskilling and continuous learning become the primary determinant of career longevity.
  • Human skills like emotional intelligence, complex ethical judgment, and high-level leadership remain resilient.

Arguments FOR (Creates Jobs / Augments)

  • AI augments human productivity, enabling workers to focus on creative strategy.
  • Creation of entirely new industries (AI safety, robotics, data audit).
  • Democratizes advanced capabilities (anyone can build or design with AI assistance).

Arguments AGAINST (Displacement / Risk)

  • Transition frictions: displaced workers rarely transition immediately into tech roles.
  • Potential widening of wealth inequality between capital owners and labor.
  • Entry-level roles in programming and writing may shrink, cutting career ladders.

Verified Facts & Statistics

  • World Economic Forum estimates 85M jobs displaced while 97M new roles emerge.
  • McKinsey predicts up to 30% of current work hours could be automated by 2030.
  • Goldman Sachs estimates generative AI could raise global GDP by 7% over 10 years.

Real-World Case Studies

  • GitHub Copilot reports developers write code up to 55% faster without replacing coders.
  • Customer service teams using Tier-1 AI bots while human agents handle complex escalations.

How to Approach This Topic in an Interview GD

Acknowledge the near-term disruption realistically, but conclude on a solution-oriented note about proactive vocational reskilling, ethical governance, and human-machine synergy.

Official Campus Placement GD Rules

To emulate professional corporate recruitment standards, every candidate must adhere to the following 10 golden rules:

1. Stay relevant to the central topic throughout.
2. Do not continuously interrupt others mid-sentence.
3. Respect other participants and their viewpoints.
4. Support assertions with verified data & examples.
5. Avoid repeating the same argument multiple times.
6. Listen actively and build directly upon prior remarks.
7. Contribute meaningful, structured value points.
8. Maintain calm, professional, non-aggressive body language.
9. Encourage quieter members and give others opportunity to speak.
10. Conclude arguments clearly with a concise forward-looking thought.
